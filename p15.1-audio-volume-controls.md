# P15.1 - Audio Volume Controls

Scope: make `renderSettings.audio.{narrationVolume,musicVolume,effectsVolume}` functional
end-to-end. Only P15.1 from `p15-audio-recon.md`. No new providers, deps, music, SFX or
subtitles; production flows, P12 provider gate, P13 runs and P14 auth untouched.

## What changed

### Volume normalization (single source of truth)
- New `lib/render/audio-mix.ts`:
  - `clampAudioVolume(value, fallback)` clamps to `[0, 4]` and rejects non-finite/non-numeric.
  - `normalizeAudioMix(audio?)` -> `AudioMixSettings` with project defaults as fallback.
  - `formatVolumeFilter(value, fallback?)` -> deterministic 3-decimal FFmpeg multiplier.
  - `audioMixesEqual(left, right)` field-wise comparison.
- `lib/projects/normalize-project.ts` now routes `renderSettings.audio` through
  `normalizeAudioMix`, so persisted/read projects always hold valid volumes (legacy
  top-level `narrationVolume`/`musicVolume` fallbacks preserved).

### Settings traced project -> RenderPlan -> CompositionPlan
- `lib/types/render.ts`: new `AudioMixSettings`; `RenderSettings.audio` reuses it;
  `RenderPlan.audio?` and `CompositionPlan.audio?` added; `CompositionArtifact.inputFingerprint.audioMix?` added.
- `lib/render/scene-resolver.ts`: `resolveRenderPlan()` populates `audio`.
- `lib/render/video-composition-service.ts`: `resolveCompositionPlan()` populates
  `audio`, and the composition artifact fingerprint records `audioMix`.

### Narration volume applied in FFmpeg
- `lib/render/video-compositor.ts`: `buildNormalizeArgs()` appends a `volume=<v>` filter
  to the scene audio chain when narration audio is the track. Existing semantics kept:
  narration -> else embedded scene audio -> else silence (`anullsrc`). Narration volume
  is never applied to silence or embedded scene audio. `musicVolume`/`effectsVolume`
  are carried but not applied yet (no music/SFX sources exist in this phase).

### Staleness
- `lib/render/render-status.ts`: `deriveCompositionArtifactStatus()` marks a composition
  `stale` ("Audio mix settings changed since the final composition was created.") when the
  recorded `audioMix` differs from the project's current normalized mix, so changed
  settings never reuse an outdated final render.

## Tests
- New `tests/audio-mix.test.ts`: clamp/normalize/format/compare.
- `tests/video-compositor-args.test.ts`: narration volume filter, custom volume, clamping,
  and no volume filter on silence/embedded audio.
- `tests/video-composition-plan.test.ts`: plan carries and normalizes the audio mix.
- `tests/render-status.test.ts`: audio mix change marks the composition stale.
- `tests/project-update.test.ts`: project update clamps out-of-range volumes.

## Remaining risks / notes
- Legacy compositions created before P15.1 have no `inputFingerprint.audioMix`; they are
  not flagged stale by a volume change until re-composed. New compositions are covered.
- `musicVolume`/`effectsVolume` are normalized, typed and fingerprinted but intentionally
  inert until music/SFX inputs land in P15.2/P15.3.
- Scene render fingerprints deliberately exclude audio mix: scene renders are
  video-only, so volumes must not force scene re-renders. Composition staleness is the
  correct invalidation point.
- FFmpeg filter-graph integration cannot be executed in this environment (no FFmpeg).
  Args construction is unit-tested; runtime mixing is covered by the existing
  FFmpeg-gated integration test when FFmpeg is present.
