## 3. Music-mix filter graph (traced)

`compose()` runs: normalize every segment → concat → (music present) mix music.
The music pass (`buildMusicMixArgs`, `video-compositor.ts:185-234`) is:

```
ffmpeg -y -i <concat> -stream_loop -1 -i <music> \
  -filter_complex \
    "[1:a]volume=<mv>,atrim=0:<T>[,afade=t=in:st=0:d=<fi>][,afade=t=out:st=<fo>:d=<fod>][music]; \
     [0:a][music]amix=inputs=2:duration=first:dropout_transition=0:normalize=0,<LIMITER>[aout]" \
  -map 0:v:0 -map [aout] -t <T> -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -movflags +faststart <out>
```

- `amix ... normalize=0` keeps the raw sum (no auto-attenuation).
- `-c:v copy` leaves the concatenated video untouched.
- Music fade-in is 1 s, fade-out 2 s (`music-mix.ts:13-14`).
- `<LIMITER>` was `alimiter=limit=0.95` (P25 left the music path unchanged so
  it kept FFmpeg's **default `level=true`**).

## 4. Root cause (Verified, local FFmpeg)

`alimiter`'s `level` option defaults to **true** ("auto level"). With
`level=true` the filter re-scales its output back toward the limit/full scale,
so the post-limiter signal sits at ≈0 dBFS instead of at the `limit`. After AAC
encoding it can even overshoot above full scale.

The P25 no-music path already used `level=false` and was safe. The music path
did not, so near-full-scale narration+music clamped to full scale.

### 4.1 Measurement methods and what they show

Measured on the same compositor-equivalent base (concatenated normalized audio,
no final limiter, as in the music path), music `volume=0.35`,
near-full-scale sources (0.9-peak narration 660 Hz + 0.9-peak music 220 Hz):

- `volumedetect` — sample peak, 0.1 dB rounding.
- `astats` — sample peak at higher precision.
- `ebur128=peak=true` — reported true peak.
- `ffmpeg -f f32le` decode + exact max/abs and count of samples `>= 1.0`
  (actual sample peaks and real clipping, independent of rounding).

### 4.2 `level=true` vs `level=false` (same fixture)

| narration vol | limiter | volumedetect | ebur128 true peak | f32 sample peak | clipped samples | decode |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `level=true` (old) | -0.0 dB | -0.0 | **-0.001 dB** | 0 | ok |
| 1 | `level=false` (new) | -0.2 dB | -0.2 | -0.221 dB | 0 | ok |
| 4 | `level=true` (old) | 0.0 dB | 0.0 | **+0.049 dB** | **18** | ok |
| 4 | `level=false` (new) | -0.4 dB | -0.4 | -0.417 dB | 0 | ok |

Interpretation:

- The 0.0 dBFS reading is **not** rounding. At narration volume 1 the f32 sample
  peak is -0.001 dB (essentially full scale); at volume 4 it is **+0.049 dB with
  18 clipped samples** — a genuine overshoot. `volumedetect`'s 0.1 dB resolution
  merely hides the small margin.
- `level=false` yields a predictable ceiling of roughly -0.2 to -0.4 dB with
  **zero** clipped samples across the tested range (volume 1 and 4).
- For these fixtures `ebur128` true peak equals the sample peak (no observed
  inter-sample overshoot), but this is content-dependent (see §8).
- Both variants decode fully to EOF.

The issue also reproduces through the real compositor (plan with music), not
only the isolated graph.

## 5. Minimal correction (implemented)

One value change, unified across both paths in `lib/render/video-compositor.ts`:

```ts
export const AUDIO_PEAK_LIMITER_FILTER = 'alimiter=limit=0.95:level=false';
```

- The music mix now uses `AUDIO_PEAK_LIMITER_FILTER` (was `alimiter=limit=0.95`).
- `buildComposeArgs` (no-music final pass, added in P25) uses the same constant.
- `limit=0.95` is unchanged, so the intended ceiling and mix balance are
  preserved; only the unintended auto-level makeup is removed.

Effects: mix structure, fades, `atrim`, stream mapping, video copy and timing
are unchanged. Below-limit content is now the pure `amix` sum with no auto
makeup gain (a ≈0.4 dB difference only for content that previously received
auto-level — corrected, not a balance redesign).

## 8. Remaining risks

1. **No true-peak guarantee (No guarantee).** `alimiter` bounds sample peaks, not
   inter-sample (true) peaks. For content with strong high-frequency energy or
   heavy lossy-codec reconstruction, the true peak after AAC can exceed the
   sample peak. Reserving extra headroom (e.g. `limit≈0.89` for ≈-1 dBFS) would
   tighten this but is an intentional, larger change left out of P25.1.
2. **Content-dependent AAC overshoot.** `level=false` measured ≈0.2-0.4 dB of
   AAC overshoot above the 0.95 (-0.45 dB) target. Comfortable but not a hard
   mathematical bound; the tested fixtures show 0 clipped samples.
3. **Adversarial-only coverage.** Fixtures are synthetic tones; real speech/music
   and perceptual quality were not evaluated (no listening tests performed).
4. **Mix level shift.** Removing auto-level makes any previously auto-levelled
   mix ≈0.4 dB quieter. Expected and correct, but observable in before/after
   comparison.
5. Timing validation (`scene.duration` vs `render.duration`), SFX,
   `effectsVolume`, stale-asset, and provider concerns remain explicitly out of
   scope (P24 §4/§7).
