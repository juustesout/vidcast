# HeyGen Stock Avatar Publication Rights

Research date: 2026-10-18. Informational only, not legal advice.

## Question

Can a video made with a HeyGen public/stock photo avatar be published publicly on
YouTube (including monetization) without additional permission?

## Sources reviewed

- HeyGen Terms of Service, effective 2025-03-06, last updated 2026-10-15:
  https://www.heygen.com/terms
- HeyGen Help Center, "Public Avatars Going Offline", updated 2025-07-03:
  https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/10047067-public-avatars-going-offline
- HeyGen Help Center, "Content ID Claims on Youtube", updated 2025-12-19:
  https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/8986144-content-id-claims-on-youtube
- HeyGen legal index:
  https://www.heygen.com/legal

## Findings

1. Paid plans get a broad output licence. The Terms state that for Creator, Pro and
   Business plans, the user owns all rights in User Input and User Output, and that
   this "does not restrict your ability to use User Output for your own purposes
   (including for commercial purposes)". The same section disclaims liability for
   commercial use.
2. The Free Plan is explicitly non-commercial. Free-plan output may not be sold,
   sublicensed, redistributed, monetized, or used in advertising, client work, or
   revenue-generating products.
3. The Terms do not contain a stock-avatar-specific licence, model release, or
   publicity-rights grant. HeyGen disclaims any warranty that output is free of
   third-party intellectual-property claims and places the risk on the user.
4. HeyGen confirms that public stock avatars exist and can be withdrawn from new
   videos, but the help article does not discuss commercial publication rights.
5. The Content ID article only covers music licensing (Pixabay/Storyblocks), not
   avatar likeness rights.
6. The Terms warn that output may not be unique and other users may generate similar
   content. Reusing a public avatar therefore also creates brand/identity ambiguity,
   even if the copyright position is acceptable.

## Conclusion

**Publication is blocked pending written confirmation from HeyGen.** The general
paid-plan output licence is useful but does not clearly cover the likeness/publicity
rights in a public stock avatar. Do not claim that the avatar is licensed for
commercial YouTube publication based on the Terms alone.

The account's plan also needs confirmation: the commercial output clause names
Creator, Pro and Business plans, while the Free Plan is non-commercial. A
pay-as-you-go API balance does not by itself prove which clause applies.

## What would unblock publication

- Written confirmation from HeyGen support that output generated with the specific
  public/stock avatar may be published and monetized on YouTube, and that HeyGen
  has the necessary model release for that use; or
- Switch to a custom/digital-twin avatar created with the presenter's own recorded
  consent, with the consent scope documented; or
- Replace the presenter with a non-likeness visual treatment.

Until then, pilot renders remain internal test artifacts and must not be published.
